# FitLife exercise library

**91 existing + 130 new = 221 exercises.** Equipment and difficulty are shown for each entry. A movement may work more than one area.

The first section lists the additions; the second lists the original exercises retained in the app. The app’s Target filter can find secondary muscles as well as the primary area shown here.

**Safety:** Start with movements appropriate for your ability. Advanced movements, loaded neck work, inversions and specialized spinal movements need particular care and may require qualified supervision. Bands, walls, bars, benches and household objects are not classified as equipment-free.

## Search names

- “Wall angels” finds **Wall Slide**; “push-up plus” finds **Scapular Push-Up**.
- “Donkey kick” finds **Quadruped Glute Kickback**; “bodyweight good morning” finds **Bodyweight Hip Hinge**.
- “Prone reverse fly” and “lying rear delt raise” find **Prone T Raise**; running/jogging and cycling are individually searchable.

Unsecured towel/sheet/door-handle rows and tree pull-ups are not included as improvised setups. Prefer a secure bar or an appropriate rated anchor. Form cues and cautions are in the app, but they are not individualized medical advice.
